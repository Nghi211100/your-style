# Your Style — Luxury Fashion Ecommerce

## 1. Vision
Your Style is a premium luxury fashion ecommerce platform. The storefront delivers an editorial, haute couture shopping experience with warm neutral tones, champagne gold accents, and photography-forward layouts. The admin dashboard provides comprehensive shop management capabilities.

**Stitch Project ID:** 7137131962138425523

## 2. Target Audience
- Fashion-conscious shoppers seeking luxury clothing
- Shop owners managing their online boutique
- Style enthusiasts browsing curated collections

## 3. Device Strategy
- Desktop-first responsive design
- All pages must work flawlessly on mobile (768px breakpoint)
- Touch-friendly interactive elements (44px minimum tap targets)
